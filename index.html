        How the Pomodoro technique works

        1. Pick one task and commit to it.
        2. Work with full focus for 25 minutes — no phone, no tabs.
        3. Take a 5-minute break: stretch, get water, look away from the screen.
        4. After 4 sessions, reward yourself with a longer 15-minute break.

              Study techniques that actually work

              🧠
              Active recall

              Close the book and test yourself. Retrieving information strengthens memory far more than re-reading.

              📆
              Spaced repetition

              Review material at increasing intervals — 1 day, 3 days, 1 week — instead of cramming.

              🍅
              Pomodoro

              Work in focused 25-minute blocks with short breaks. Try it with the Study Timer.

              👩‍🏫
              Feynman technique

              Explain a concept in simple words as if teaching a child. Gaps in your explanation reveal gaps in understanding.

              🔀
              Interleaving

              Mix different topics or problem types in one session rather than blocking a single topic.

              😴
              Sleep & breaks

              Memory consolidates during sleep. An all-nighter usually costs more than it earns.
